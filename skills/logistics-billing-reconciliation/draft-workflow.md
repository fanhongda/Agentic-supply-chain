Decide which transportation service model is used for the billed invoice.
- Check the service provide name, If DHL Express, FedEx, Shun Feng, it is express.
- Type of service: EXPRESS then go to express check; LCL then go to SEA LCL check; FCL then go to FCL check.
- If the billing VESSEL, OCEAN BILL OF LANDING, HOUSE BILL OF LANDING, then it is for SEA shipment.
- If airway bill, then go to AIR FREIGHT CHECK.
