  skill, Claude mostly reaches a sensible answer but does not follow the report template,

- **c12 at Δ +0.00 is the right answer, not a gap.** Both arms score 1.00: a general
  shipping question gets a general answer either way. A skill that improved this case

  0.78 and 1.00 across runs. Judging "did it correctly decline to conclude" is the
  fuzziest call in the suite, so treat anything ≥ 0.78 on these two as passing and watch
  the trend rather than the single figure. The deterministic graders hold steady on both.
